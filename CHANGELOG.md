# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- On fair day, enter the fruit contest for 100 Bells and get a thank-you letter. Apple Day mails a letter of its own. A setting picks the daily letter's present, and other mods can ask whether the fair is on.

## 1.0.0

- An example mod from the Play AC Online team: two calendar days with a letter template, and a script that mails a letter once a game day. Its files show data and a script in one mod.
